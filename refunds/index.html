Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains when we refund a charge for Oxx Report. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Pay as you use

You pay for the tokens you use. If a report fails or cannot be used because of a fault on our side, we will regenerate it or refund the tokens it used. A draft that needs your review and edits is not a fault: professional review is part of the workflow.

Monthly subscription

You can cancel at any time. Your subscription then ends at the close of the current billing month, and you keep access until then. We do not refund part months or token usage already used.

Billing mistakes

If we charge you the wrong amount or charge you twice, we refund the difference in full.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@oxx-ai.com within 30 days of the charge, with the date, the amount and the reason. If we approve the refund, we pay it to your original payment method within 30 days.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.
