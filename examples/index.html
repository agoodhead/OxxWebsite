How It Works

One reporting workflow from inspection to review.

Oxx Report keeps the work moving through five practical stages: capture the evidence, organise the context, ground the reasoning, generate the draft, and review the result.

01

Inspect / Capture

Capture photos, voice notes, typed notes, site observations, historic files, and client context before the information gets lost.

02

Organise

Keep evidence, notes, photos, documents, and observations linked to the case instead of reconstructing the report from scattered files.

03

Ground the Reasoning

Connect observations to defects, components, standards, clauses, and related findings before report drafting begins.

04

Generate Report

Generate a structured professional first draft with selected evidence, captions, findings, template structure, audience, tone, and report length.

05

Review and Refine

Review the generated draft, edit sections, refine language, verify reasoning, and approve the final report under professional responsibility.
