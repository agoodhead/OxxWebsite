# Oxx Report Features

## Workflow Features

- Site inspection capture
- Voice notes, typed notes, photo capture, and document capture
- Case-based organisation of evidence and context
- Observation, defect, and component grouping
- Standards and clause reference support
- Template-based report generation
- Photo selection, captions, headings, and layout support
- Review and refinement before professional approval

## Trust Guardrails

- Oxx Report does not replace professional judgment.
- Draft reports require professional review.
- The professional remains responsible for final approval.
- The workflow is intended to reduce repetitive administration, not remove accountability.

## Typical Outputs

- Structured inspection records
- Organised evidence and observations
- Draft findings and report sections
- Draft reports tailored to audience, tone, length, and template
- Review-ready Word-based reporting material
