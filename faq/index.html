FAQ

Documentation for the Oxx Report workflow.

Short answers on what the workflow does, where professional review fits, and what platform access looks like.

Oxx Report is an AI-assisted reporting workflow tool for inspection, consultation, assessment, and technical reporting work. It turns site photos, voice notes, and observations into a structured draft report that the professional reviews and approves.

Engineers, inspectors, consultants, and other professionals who write inspection, assessment, and technical reports and want a faster first draft without giving up final sign-off.

The workflow is Inspect / Capture, Organise, Ground the Reasoning, Generate Report, and Review / Refine.

Photos, voice notes, and observations are captured on site straight into the report case and stay linked to it. Oxx Report groups the evidence, connects it to defects, components, and standards, and generates a structured first draft with sections, captions, and findings. The engineer then reviews, edits, and approves the report.
