Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Oxx Report handles personal information on oxx-ai.com and in the Oxx Report app at report.oxx-ai.com. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

Oxx Report is operated by OXX AI, 1 Albion Springs Cl, 41 Albion Place, Rondebosch, Western Cape, 7700, South Africa. Send privacy questions and requests to support@oxx-ai.com.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you sign up or contact us, such as your name and email address.
  • Billing details needed to charge you.
  • Content you add to the app, such as photos, voice notes, notes and documents, and the reports made from them.
  • Messages you send to the chat assistant on our FAQ page.

The app stores only what you enter or upload.

Information about other people

Your content may include personal information about other people, such as property owners or people in photos. You decide what to upload and must have a lawful reason to collect it. For that information you are the responsible party and we act as your operator under POPIA: we process it only to provide the service to you.

How we use it

  • To run the app and produce your draft reports.
  • To bill you and keep financial records.
  • To answer your questions and support you.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run Oxx Report, such as hosting, payment and AI model providers, and where the law requires it. We only use providers that are bound by law or agreement to protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies or tracking. It only stores a note in your browser so it knows when to show the install prompt.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you close your account or ask us to delete your information, we delete it, except records the law requires us to keep, such as invoices.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your personal information. Email support@oxx-ai.com. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
